Moving from fips 0.1#

fips 0.2 replaces the pandas-backed structures of 0.1 (Block, Vector, Matrix, CovarianceMatrix, InverseProblem and the estimators) with labeled linear algebra: a Space of labels, values on it (Vector), matrices between spaces (Operator, Covariance), a Problem that checks its labels once, and fips.solve(), which returns a Posterior. Each table below gives a name of 0.1.0b9 and what replaces it, by topic. The numbers do not change: the analytic method gives the posterior the Bayesian estimator gave.

Five changes of behavior to know before the tables:

  • The inputs are matched by label, exactly. A forward operator, a covariance or an offset must have exactly the labels of the observations or of the prior, in any order. InverseProblem filled the labels a matrix lacked with zeros (with a “Partial overlap” warning) and dropped the extra ones; a Problem raises and names them. A forward operator that covers only some cells is put on the whole state by Operator.from_blocks (a label it lacks is zero), or the state is cut to what it covers with Problem.restrict.

  • Labels are kept as given. 0.1 converted labels to numbers (site codes "010" became 10) and InverseProblem rounded float labels to 6 decimals. Neither happens now; round on purpose with round_labels(6).

  • Covariances take standard deviations. The error components took variances; a model’s with_std takes their square roots.

  • The problem’s arguments are in a new order and have new names: Problem(prior, prior_cov, obs, obs_cov, forward, offset=None), where InverseProblem was (obs, prior, forward_operator, modeldata_mismatch, prior_error, constant=None). Pass them by name.

  • A problem is not solved in place. fips.solve(problem) returns the posterior and leaves the problem as it was.

Pickles written by 0.1 do not load in 0.2. The words of the new names are defined in Terminology.
